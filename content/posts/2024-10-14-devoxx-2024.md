I was given a chance to attend [Devoxx Genesis 2024](https://devoxx.be/), which was held in Antwerp, Belgium from October 7th to 11th.
I've already been to Devoxx UK back in 2019, but this was my first Devoxx in Belgium.

It was a 5 day conference with 2 days of deep dives and workshops, and 3 days of talks. Together with other attendees we estimated that a third of all talks was related to AI/LLMs. A bit too much in my opinion. But there were enough of other interesting talks.

In the next days, I'll be posting articles from Devox talks/worshops that I found to be most interesting. In the meantime, here are some highlights of Antwerpen.

We flew to Brussels and took a train to Antwerpen. The following view awaited us after arriving to Antwerpen central station.
